### Email 2 (Day 2) — Why construction CVs fail screening
**Subject:** Why good engineers fail CV screening

Hi [First name],

After 29 years in construction, here's the pattern I see in CVs that struggle:

1. **The words aren't there.** You coordinated temporary works for years, but "temporary works" never appears. Recruiter searches find words, not implications.
2. **Evidence is buried.** Your best project is a paragraph on page three. Recruiters scan; it might as well not exist.
3. **Format fights the parser.** Two-column template, contact details in a header graphic, skill bars. Screening software reads it badly — or not at all.

None of this is about your ability. It's about presentation — and presentation is fixable.

Tomorrow's email shows the single highest-impact fix: project evidence.

David

### Email 3 (Day 5) — How to show project evidence
**Subject:** The bullet formula recruiters trust

Hi [First name],

Compare:

> "Responsible for temporary works on site."

> "Coordinated temporary works on a £40m highways scheme — 25+ design briefs managed to BS 5975, zero non-conformances."

Same person. Same job. Different bullet.

The formula: **action + what + scale + standard/method + outcome.** Every element truthful and defensible at interview.

The Project Achievement Worksheet in your pack walks you through this for each role. Do your three most recent projects this week — that's usually 80% of the improvement.

David

### Email 4 (Day 8) — LinkedIn profile mistakes
**Subject:** Recruiters are searching LinkedIn right now. Are you findable?

Hi [First name],

The three LinkedIn mistakes I see most in construction:

1. **Headline = job title.** "Engineer at [Company]" wastes your most searchable field. Use role + specialism + sector.
2. **Empty experience entries.** A title and dates tells a recruiter nothing. Two lines plus project evidence per role.
3. **CV mismatch.** Different titles or dates between CV and LinkedIn quietly kills trust. Recruiters always cross-check.

The LinkedIn Profile Checklist in your pack covers all of it, section by section.

David

- Always honour unsubscribes immediately; only email people who opted in (checklist consent checkbox) or are customers.
