# 30-Day Launch Plan — Construction Career Edge

Goal state by Day 30: website live · Stripe products live · CV review form live · lead magnet live · LinkedIn profile updated · 10 blog drafts created · 30 LinkedIn posts prepared · 100 target prospects listed · first email campaign written · first paid service ready.

## Week 1 — Foundations (Days 1–7)
- **Day 1:** Buy the domain **linkedinconstructioncvprofile.com** (already configured in the site). Credentials confirmed: MSc Construction Management · PMI-CP · IEng (ICE).
- **Day 2:** Deploy the site to Vercel (docs/DEPLOYMENT.md). Connect the domain + SSL.
- **Day 3:** Create Stripe account, 6 products and Payment Links; paste into `src/lib/site.ts`; set real prices (docs/STRIPE-SETUP.md). Run a test-mode payment.
- **Day 4:** Set up Formspree (3 forms) and Calendly; paste IDs into `site.ts` (docs/FORMS-AND-BOOKING-SETUP.md). Test the upload form end-to-end with a real file.
- **Day 5:** Fill legal page placeholders (trading details, retention periods, dates). Arrange legal review. Set up the business mailbox with 2FA + folders.
- **Day 6:** Photography: professional headshot + 2–3 site-appropriate images (own archive or licensed stock). Replace `ImagePlaceholder` components.
- **Day 7:** Full site QA on mobile and desktop: every link, every form, every Stripe button. Submit sitemap to Google Search Console.

## Week 2 — Content & profile (Days 8–14)
- **Day 8:** Rewrite David's LinkedIn profile using docs/LINKEDIN-PLAN.md (headline, About, Featured).
- **Day 9:** Set up the email tool (MailerLite/ConvertKit); connect the checklist form; load Sequence A from docs/EMAIL-FUNNELS.md.
- **Day 10:** Load Sequence B (customer onboarding) as templates. Write the launch announcement email.
- **Days 11–13:** Draft blog articles 1–6 from docs/BLOG-SEO-PLAN.md (pillar pieces first: #1, #31 variants, #36, #41).
- **Day 14:** Draft blog articles 7–10. Schedule the first two for publication.

## Week 3 — Audience building (Days 15–21)
- **Day 15:** Prepare the 30 LinkedIn posts (docs/LINKEDIN-PLAN.md §2) in a scheduler or document.
- **Day 16:** Build the 100-prospect list: 50 construction recruiters + 50 warm contacts (ex-colleagues, site connections, group members). Spreadsheet: name, role, sector, relationship, last contact.
- **Day 17:** **Soft launch.** Publish LinkedIn post #1 (announcement). Tell 10 closest industry contacts directly.
- **Days 18–21:** Daily LinkedIn post + 20 min engagement. Send 5 recruiter connection requests/day (templates in LINKEDIN-PLAN.md). Join 6 construction groups.

## Week 4 — Revenue & iteration (Days 22–30)
- **Day 22:** Send the launch email campaign to any existing list/contacts (template #4 in MARKETING-COPY.md §5).
- **Day 23:** Offer 2–3 founding-client CV reviews at an early-bird rate in exchange for honest, permissioned testimonials (never fabricated).
- **Days 24–26:** Deliver founding work; refine the questionnaire and process docs from real friction.
- **Day 27:** Publish blog articles 3–4. Post the free checklist to relevant groups (within group rules).
- **Day 28:** Collect first testimonials (templates §7) and add to the site with permission.
- **Day 29:** Review analytics: traffic sources, form starts vs completions, Stripe clicks. Fix the biggest drop-off.
- **Day 30:** Retro + next-30 plan: publishing cadence, outreach targets, price review after first 5 paid orders.

## Weekly rhythm after Day 30
- 1–2 blog posts/week (plan in docs/BLOG-SEO-PLAN.md)
- 4–5 LinkedIn posts/week + daily 20-min engagement
- 5 new recruiter/prospect connections/week
- Email list: 1 value email/fortnight minimum
- Monthly: pipeline review, testimonial requests, price/packaging review
