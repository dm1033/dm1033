## Publishing guidance

- **Cadence:** 1–2 articles/week; pillar pieces (#1, #31, #36, #41) first.
- **Internal linking:** every article links to one service page + one related article + one lead magnet.
- **Format:** H1 = title, intro states the reader's problem, H2 sections, honest disclaimer where screening/outcomes are discussed, CTA block at the end (the `ArticleLayout` component does this automatically).
- **Compliance:** never promise interviews, offers, ATS passes or salary outcomes in titles or meta descriptions.
