## 3. Connection message templates

1. (Engineer/manager) "Hi [Name] — I post practical CV and career content for construction professionals (29 years in the industry myself). Connecting so it reaches your feed. No pitch."
2. (After they engage) "Hi [Name], thanks for the comment on my post about [topic]. What's your situation — happy where you are, or thinking about a move?"
3. (Recruiter) "Hi [Name] — I prep construction candidates' CVs and LinkedIn profiles. Useful contact in both directions, I suspect. No partnership claims, just a sensible connection."
4. (Graduate) "Hi [Name] — saw you're heading into [discipline]. I post free first-CV guidance for construction grads; connect if useful."
5. (Former colleague) "Hi [Name], long time since [project/company]. I've moved into construction career coaching and CV work — would value the reconnect either way."

## 4. Recruiter networking plan
- Build a list of 50 construction recruiters: civils, infrastructure, temporary works, M&E, energy, Gulf/SG specialists (LinkedIn search + agency sites).
- Connect with 5/week using template 3 — never pitch in the first message.
- Engage with their posts before and after connecting (genuine comments).
- Monthly: ask one recruiter one research question (see outreach templates §6 in MARKETING-COPY.md) — builds the relationship and your content.
- Offer value first: refer candidates to recruiters where appropriate; the reverse follows naturally.
- Never claim a recruiter partnership publicly unless agreed in writing.

## 5. Construction group posting strategy
- Join 6–10 active groups: civil engineering, temporary works, construction management, QS/commercial, construction careers, region-specific (UAE/SG) groups.
- Read each group's rules; many ban direct promotion.
- Post the educational content (tips, myth-busts) — not service offers.
- Answer CV/career questions in comments generously; the profile click is the funnel.
- One genuinely useful contribution per group per week beats daily spam.
