# Interview STAR Answer Worksheet

Prepare structured, truthful answers to construction interview questions.
STAR = Situation, Task, Action, Result.

## How to use
Pick 6-8 real situations from your career covering: safety, programme
pressure, technical problem, conflict, leadership, commercial issue, mistake
you fixed. Complete one block per situation.

## STAR block (copy per answer)
SITUATION - Project, your role, the context (2 sentences):

TASK - What specifically needed doing, and why it mattered:

ACTION - What YOU did (not "we") - decisions, steps, who you involved:

RESULT - Outcome with truthful numbers where possible
(programme saved, cost avoided, audit passed, zero incidents):

## Common construction interview questions to prepare
1. Talk me through a project you're proud of.
2. Describe a time you stopped work for safety reasons.
3. Tell me about a programme slippage you helped recover.
4. Describe a disagreement with a subcontractor and how you resolved it.
5. Tell me about a technical problem you solved on site.
6. Describe a time you managed a temporary works issue.
7. What's the biggest mistake you've made and what changed afterwards?
8. Why this role, and why us?

## Rules
- Real situations only — interviewers probe for detail
- Practise saying answers aloud in under 2 minutes
- End each answer with the result, not the story
