# LinkedIn Profile Checklist for Construction Professionals

Advisory checklist — recruiter behaviour varies and visibility cannot be
guaranteed.

## Headline
- More than your job title: role + specialism + sector
- Example: "Senior Site Engineer | Highways & Structures | Temporary Works | Setting Out"
- Uses words recruiters genuinely search

## Photo and banner
- Clear, professional head-and-shoulders photo
- Banner image relevant to your sector (site, infrastructure, project)

## About section
- First two lines earn the click ("see more" cuts the rest)
- Your specialism, sectors, project types and direction — in first person
- Honest keywords woven naturally, not listed

## Experience
- Titles and dates match your CV exactly
- Each role: 2-4 lines + project evidence bullets
- Name methods, standards, software and sectors truthfully

## Skills
- Up to 50 allowed — prioritise the searchable, role-relevant ones
- Pin your top 3 to match your target role

## Featured section
- Add: project photos (where permitted), certificates, articles, portfolio

## Settings
- "Open to work" set for recruiters (visible to recruiters only if employed)
- Location and industry set correctly
- Custom URL claimed (linkedin.com/in/yourname)

## Activity
- Comment on industry posts weekly — visibility compounds
- Connect with recruiters in your sector with a short, honest note
