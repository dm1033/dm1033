# Construction Keywords by Role

Keyword THEMES recruiters search for, by role family. Use a term only if it
truthfully reflects your experience — keyword stuffing and false claims are
unethical and easily exposed.

## Site / Civil Engineer
Setting out, GPS/total station, earthworks, drainage, reinforced concrete,
ITPs, QA records, as-builts, RAMS, permits to dig, NEC4, Civil 3D, AutoCAD.

## Temporary Works (TWC / TWS / Designer)
BS 5975, design brief, design check categories, falsework, formwork, propping,
excavation support, scaffold interface, lifting plans, TW register, PAS 8811/8812.

## Site / Construction Manager
SMSTS, programme management, subcontractor management, short-term planning,
quality assurance, safety leadership, toolbox talks, commercial awareness, handover.

## Project Manager
NEC4/JCT administration, programme (P6/Asta), risk management, early warnings,
compensation events, stakeholder management, cost control, reporting, governance.

## Quantity Surveyor
NEC4/JCT, valuations, variations, CVRs, subcontract procurement, final accounts,
cash flow, measurement (NRM/CESMM), commercial reporting.

## Planner
Primavera P6, Asta Powerproject, baseline, critical path, EOT, delay analysis,
4D planning, progress reporting, tender programmes.

## H&S / HSE
NEBOSH, IOSH, CDM 2015, RAMS review, audits, incident investigation,
behavioural safety, CSCS, safety leadership, ISO 45001.

## HV / Energy / Utilities
HV substations, 11kV-400kV, cable installation, jointing interfaces, DNO,
National Grid, SSE/UKPN, outage management, commissioning, SAP/AP (state scheme).

## BIM / Design Management
BIM coordination, Revit, Navisworks, clash detection, ISO 19650, CDE,
design review, RFIs, buildability.
