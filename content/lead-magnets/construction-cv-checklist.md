# Construction CV Checklist (20 Points)

Construction Career Edge — advisory guide. No checklist can guarantee
interviews or selection; final decisions remain with employers and recruiters.

## Structure
1. Name and contact details in the body text (not a header graphic)
2. 3-4 line professional profile: who you are, specialism, target role
3. Standard headings: Profile, Key Skills, Experience, Education
4. Reverse chronological experience with clear date ranges
5. Two focused pages; older roles summarised briefly

## Evidence
6. Every recent role shows at least one concrete project example
7. Project examples cover: project type, scale, your responsibility, outcome
8. Measurable detail where truthful: value bands, durations, quantities, team sizes
9. Achievements, not duty lists ("delivered X" beats "responsible for X")
10. Temporary works, HV, rail, safety or sector specialisms named explicitly

## Keywords (honestly)
11. Target job title appears truthfully in your profile line
12. Methods, standards and software you genuinely use are named (e.g. BS 5975, P6, AutoCAD)
13. Qualifications written in full AND abbreviated (Temporary Works Coordinator (TWC))
14. Wording aligned to the advert's terminology where genuinely applicable
15. No keyword stuffing, no hidden text, no invented experience — ever

## Formatting
16. Single-column, simple layout for portal submissions
17. No text boxes, skill bars, photos or graphics in the ATS version
18. Standard font, 10-12pt, consistent spacing
19. Dates consistent with your LinkedIn profile
20. Saved as a clean PDF for humans and a plain Word/PDF version for portals
