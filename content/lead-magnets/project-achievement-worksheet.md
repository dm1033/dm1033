## Step 5 — Write the bullet
Formula: Action verb + what + scale + standard/method + outcome.

Weak:   "Responsible for temporary works on site."
Strong: "Coordinated temporary works on a major highways scheme,
         managing 25+ design briefs to BS 5975 with zero non-conformances."

Rules: every word defensible at interview. No invented values. No inflation.
