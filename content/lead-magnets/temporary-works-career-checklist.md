# Temporary Works Career Profile Checklist

Evidencing TWC / TWS / TW design experience for your CV and LinkedIn.
Advisory only — appointments and competence decisions rest with employers.

## Core evidence to capture
- Formal appointments held: TWS, TWC (and appointing organisation)
- Training: CITB TWCTC / TWSTC, employer schemes, refreshers and dates
- BS 5975 working knowledge - and PAS 8811/8812 where applicable
- TW registers owned or maintained (how many live items?)
- Design briefs raised/managed (volume, complexity)
- Design check categories handled (0-3) and interfaces with designers

## Scheme types (name what you've genuinely done)
Falsework, formwork, excavation support, propping and needling, scaffold
interfaces, crane/piling platforms (incl. BRE 470 awareness), hoarding,
temporary stability, lifting operations interfaces, cofferdams, bridge jacking.

## CV presentation
- Put "Temporary Works" in your profile line and skills if it's your route
- Each role: name the TW scope explicitly - searches find words, not implications
- Quantify truthfully: "managed TW register of 60+ items across 3 sites"
- Show the safety dimension: inspections, permits to load/strike, audits

## LinkedIn
- Headline includes "Temporary Works" if it's your specialism or target
- About section: one paragraph on your TW philosophy and scope
- Skills: Temporary Works, BS 5975, Falsework, Formwork, Excavation Support

## Moving INTO temporary works?
- Capture every TW interface you've already had (briefs raised, checks chased)
- Get the training booked - then evidence it immediately
- A coaching call can map your specific route
