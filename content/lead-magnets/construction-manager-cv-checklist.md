# Construction Manager CV Checklist

What a construction / site manager CV must show. Advisory checklist —
no format guarantees selection.

## The four pillars (every CM CV needs all four)
1. DELIVERY - programmes met, sections handed over, milestones achieved
2. SAFETY - leadership evidence: audits, stand-downs, improvement, stats where truthful
3. COMMERCIAL - subcontractor management, cost awareness, variations, reporting
4. PEOPLE - team sizes led, subcontractors coordinated, development of juniors

## Checklist
- Profile states scale honestly: project types and value bands you've run
- Each project entry: type, value band, client, your exact accountability
- Programme evidence: "delivered 14-week section on time despite X"
- Safety qualifications clear: SMSTS, first aid, sector tickets, IOSH/NEBOSH
- Quality: handover/defect record, ITP regimes, NCR closure
- Commercial literacy: CVR input, early warnings, procurement involvement
- Stakeholders: client interface, public, designers, utilities, rail/DNO possessions
- Systems named: P6/Asta exposure, field view/quality apps, CDM duties
- Team evidence: direct reports, peak labour, subcontract packages
- Two pages; last 10 years detailed, earlier roles summarised

## Red flags to remove
- "Responsible for the day-to-day running of the site" (says nothing)
- Duties copied from a job description
- No numbers anywhere (or numbers you can't defend)
- Title inflation - reference checks will find it
