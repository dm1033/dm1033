# Temporary Works Design — Engagement Contract

**Template — version 0.1**
**Status:** Draft for solicitor review before use on live projects.

> **Important.** This is a working template prepared as a starting point for engagements between Temporary Works Consulting & Design Ltd and its clients. It is **not legal advice** and is **not** ready for use without review by a qualified solicitor familiar with English construction-consultancy contracts (and, where relevant, the jurisdiction of the client). It should be tailored to each engagement using **Schedule 1 (Scope)** and **Schedule 2 (Fees)**. Replace every `[bracketed]` placeholder before signing.

## 1. Definitions and interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, the following words have the meanings set out below:

- **"Agreement"** — this engagement contract, including its Schedules.
- **"Business Day"** — a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in England.
- **"Client Information"** — drawings, specifications, ground information, plant data, programmes, surveys, registers, design briefs, instructions and any other information provided by or on behalf of the Client.
- **"Deliverables"** — the calculations, drawings, reports, design briefs, RAMS, ITPs, comments, schedules or other documents to be produced by the Consultant under this Agreement, as listed in Schedule 1.
- **"Fees"** — the fees payable by the Client to the Consultant under this Agreement, as set out in Schedule 2.
- **"Project"** — the project described in Schedule 1.
- **"Services"** — the services described in Schedule 1, to be performed by the Consultant.
- **"Standards"** — the standards and guidance referenced in Schedule 1 (which may include BS 5975:2024, BS EN 1990–1997, BRE 470, applicable TWf and CIRIA guidance and any client- or contract-specific procedures).

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation. References to "including" mean "including without limitation". The Schedules form part of this Agreement.

## 2. Services

2.1 The Consultant shall perform the Services with the reasonable skill, care and diligence to be expected of a competent professional providing services of a similar nature in the same field.

2.2 The Services are advisory and engineering services only. The Consultant is **not** appointed as the Client's Temporary Works Coordinator, Temporary Works Supervisor, Principal Designer or Principal Contractor and accepts no role under the Construction (Design and Management) Regulations 2015 unless expressly agreed in writing in Schedule 1.

2.3 The Services are to be performed remotely and on a project basis unless otherwise agreed. Site visits, witness inspections or attendance are not included unless expressly listed in Schedule 1.

2.4 **Categories of design check.** Any design check provided is at the category stated in Schedule 1 (CAT 1, CAT 2 or CAT 3 as defined by reference to BS 5975:2024). Where no category is stated, the parties shall agree it in writing before the check begins.

2.5 **Excluded services.** Unless expressly listed in Schedule 1, the Services do not include:

(a) Permanent works structural design or design verification;
(b) Statutory inspections, building control or third-party approvals;
(c) On-site supervision, witnessing or signing off works;
(d) Acting as Principal Designer, Principal Contractor, Temporary Works Coordinator or Temporary Works Supervisor;
(e) Geotechnical investigation, surveying or testing;
(f) Procurement, programming, cost or commercial advice;
(g) Legal, regulatory or insurance advice.

---

## 3. Client Information and assumptions

3.1 The Client shall provide, at no cost to the Consultant and in good time, all Client Information reasonably required for the Services. The Consultant is entitled to rely on the accuracy, completeness and currency of Client Information.

3.2 Deliverables are produced subject to the assumptions stated in them and to the Standards referenced in Schedule 1. They are **subject to project-specific information** and must be re-confirmed or revised if any Client Information changes materially.

3.3 The Client shall notify the Consultant promptly of any change in scope, programme, ground conditions, plant, loads or other circumstances that may affect the Services or Deliverables.

---

## 4. Programme

4.1 The Consultant shall use reasonable endeavours to perform the Services in line with the dates and durations stated in Schedule 1, subject to receipt of Client Information and instructions.

4.2 The Consultant is not liable for delays caused by the Client, by third parties not under the Consultant's control, by changes in scope or by events of force majeure.

---

## 5. Fees and payment

5.1 The Fees are payable as set out in Schedule 2. Unless stated otherwise, Fees are exclusive of VAT and reasonable disbursements.

5.2 Invoices are payable within **[30] days** of the invoice date by bank transfer to the account stated on the invoice. The Consultant may charge interest on overdue sums under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998.

5.3 The Consultant may suspend performance of the Services if any invoice remains unpaid more than **[14] days** beyond its due date, having given the Client at least **[7] days'** prior written notice.

5.4 Deliverables, calculations, drawings and any associated rights are issued on the basis that all Fees due to the Consultant in respect of those Deliverables have been paid. Unpaid Deliverables may not be used or relied upon by the Client.

---

## 6. Variations

6.1 Either Party may propose a change to the Services in writing. A change takes effect only when the Parties have agreed it in writing, including any adjustment to Fees and programme.

---

## 7. Intellectual property

7.1 The Consultant retains all intellectual property rights in the Deliverables, calculation methods, templates and tools used or developed in the course of the Services.

7.2 On full payment of the Fees relating to a Deliverable, the Consultant grants the Client a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free licence to use that Deliverable for the purpose of the Project only.

7.3 The Client shall not use the Deliverables on any other project, share them with third parties (other than the Client's professional team for the Project) or modify them without the Consultant's prior written consent.

---

## 8. Confidentiality and data protection

8.1 Each Party shall keep confidential any information disclosed by the other that is marked as confidential or is reasonably regarded as such, and shall use it only for the purposes of this Agreement.

8.2 Confidentiality does not apply to information that is or becomes public other than through breach of this Agreement, was lawfully known before disclosure, is independently developed or is required to be disclosed by law or a regulator.

8.3 Each Party shall comply with applicable data protection laws (including the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) in relation to any personal data shared under this Agreement.

---

## 9. Liability and insurance

9.1 The Consultant shall maintain throughout the term of this Agreement, and for a period of **[6] years** after completion of the Services:

(a) Professional Indemnity insurance of not less than **£[insert limit]** any one claim (or annual aggregate, as applicable); and
(b) Public Liability insurance of not less than **£[insert limit]** any one claim.

The Consultant shall provide a certificate of insurance on reasonable request.

9.2 **Cap on liability.** Subject to clause 9.4, the total aggregate liability of the Consultant to the Client arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), breach of statutory duty or otherwise, shall not exceed the greater of:

(a) the total Fees paid by the Client to the Consultant under this Agreement; and
(b) **£[insert cap, e.g. £1,000,000]**,

and shall in any event be limited to the amount actually recoverable under the Consultant's Professional Indemnity insurance for the claim concerned.

9.3 **Excluded losses.** The Consultant shall not be liable for any indirect or consequential loss, or for any loss of profit, loss of revenue, loss of business, loss of contract, loss of opportunity, loss of use, loss of goodwill, or loss or corruption of data.

9.4 **Liabilities not excluded.** Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes any liability that cannot be limited or excluded by law, including liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation.

9.5 **Reliance.** The Deliverables are provided for the benefit of the Client and for the Project only. The Consultant accepts no duty of care to any third party in respect of the Deliverables, save where a collateral warranty has been executed by the Consultant.

9.6 **Net contribution.** The liability of the Consultant for any loss or damage shall be limited to that proportion which would be just and equitable having regard to the responsibility of any other party who may be liable for the same loss or damage, on the assumption that such other party has paid to the Client such proportion which it would be just and equitable for that party to pay.

---

## 10. Termination

10.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on **[30] days'** written notice.

10.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other:

(a) commits a material breach which is not remedied within **[14] days** of written notice; or
(b) becomes insolvent, enters administration or ceases or threatens to cease to carry on business.

10.3 On termination, the Client shall pay the Consultant for all Services performed and disbursements incurred up to the date of termination.

10.4 Clauses dealing with intellectual property, confidentiality, liability, insurance, governing law and any other clauses intended to survive shall survive termination.

---

## 11. Notices

11.1 Notices must be in writing and sent to the contact persons and addresses stated in Schedule 1. Notices by email are valid where receipt is acknowledged by the recipient.

---

## 12. General

12.1 This Agreement (and the Schedules) is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject and supersedes any prior agreement, representation or understanding.

12.2 No amendment is valid unless made in writing and signed by both Parties.

12.3 Failure or delay in exercising any right is not a waiver of that right.

12.4 If any provision is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remainder shall continue in force.

12.5 The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship.

12.6 Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement without the other Party's written consent (not to be unreasonably withheld).

12.7 A person who is not a Party has no rights under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999.

---

## 13. Governing law and jurisdiction

13.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of **England and Wales**.

13.2 The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of **England and Wales**, save that the Consultant may bring proceedings in any court of competent jurisdiction in respect of unpaid Fees.

> *For engagements through the LLC, replace "England and Wales" with the agreed jurisdiction (typically the law of the State of Delaware, USA, with non-exclusive jurisdiction of the Delaware courts, or as agreed with the Client).*
