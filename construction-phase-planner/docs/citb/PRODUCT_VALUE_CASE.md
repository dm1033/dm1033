# Product Value Case — Construction Phase Safety Planner

*"Plan the Site. Manage the Risk. Defend Every Decision."*

**Status:** SMSTS-aligned construction planning and safety simulation, presented to CITB for review and consideration. Supports training and assessment; does not replace formal CITB course delivery, examination or certification. **All financial figures in this document are illustrative** — the product is pre-pilot and no proven-savings claims are made.

---

## 1. Where the value comes from

### 1.1 Reduced printing and paper
The simulation replaces printed workbooks, case-study packs and paper site-plan exercises with a browser-based exercise. It is designed to be paperless end-to-end: exercises on screen, evidence exported as PDF (browser print) and CSV. Packs never date in the print room — scenario content is data-driven JSON updated centrally.

### 1.2 Reduced marking and admin time
The scoring engine marks as the delegate plays: decisions (with partial credit), site-plan placements, a temporary works register marked per field, permit selections, and incident responses. At the end of a run the tutor receives, automatically: a score report, a decision log against best answers, a missed-items report, a model-answer overlay, a tutor review sheet and a per-outcome coverage report. The hand-marking and evidence-collation steps of a paper exercise largely disappear; the tutor's time moves to discussion and coaching (supported by the tutor console's pause and reveal-answers functions).

### 1.3 Remote and low-infrastructure delivery
Fully static, no installation, no accounts. Runs in any modern browser; offline-capable in classrooms after first load via service worker; deployable from any static host or a folder on a laptop. A provider can run the same exercise in a training room with no Wi-Fi and on a remote course, identically.

### 1.4 Measurable evidence of understanding
This is the differentiator. Ten SMSTS-aligned learning outcomes are mapped to deterministic gameplay steps in every scenario, enforced by a data validator — a delegate cannot complete a scenario without addressing every outcome. Every run yields per-outcome coverage and performance (RAG), plus the full decision log. For internal quality assurance — and any external scrutiny — the provider holds per-delegate, per-outcome evidence rather than a signed attendance sheet and a marked worksheet.

### 1.5 Engagement
Delegates manage a project rather than complete a worksheet: consequences move live meters, unsafe choices are logged as critical failures, and three incident events are drawn at random per run so no two runs are identical. Automated end-to-end testing confirms all three scenarios play through cleanly; engagement outcomes (attention, discussion quality, knowledge retention) are exactly what the pilot is designed to measure — we do not claim them yet.

- **Cost out:** printing eliminated; marking and collation largely automated. Illustrative model above; your figures will differ — use your own.
