## Writing rules across all posts

- One H1 per article matching the SEO title.
- Use H2s for main sections, H3s for sub-points.
- Internal-link 2–4 service pages per post.
- Add a 3-question FAQ block at the end (use FAQPage JSON-LD on individual blog posts where the questions are unique).
- Use cautious language: "in accordance with relevant principles", "subject to project-specific information".
- Don't name clients or share project drawings without written permission.
