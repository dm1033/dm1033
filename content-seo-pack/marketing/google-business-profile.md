Temporary Works Consulting provides specialist Temporary Works Design, CAT 1/2/3 design checking and construction engineering support for UK and international projects.

The service is led personally by David, a UK civil engineer and Temporary Works Designer with over 28 years' experience across construction, civils, safety and training.

Services include Temporary Works Design (calculations and drawings), independent CAT 3 design checking, BRE 470 piling mat and crane mat design, excavation support, hoardings, propping, RAMS, ITPs and BS 5975:2024 procedure support.

A free online BRE 470 piling mat design tool is also available at bre470pilingmatdesign.com.
