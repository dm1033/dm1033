## Variant B — Why I built the BRE 470 tool

There's no shortage of working platforms that get specified late, sized roughly, and then become a problem on site.

I built bre470pilingmatdesign.com to give engineers and contractors a quick, indicative BRE 470 piling mat sizing — useful at tender, pre-construction and as a working aid during design.

It doesn't replace project-specific design — that still has to consider site, ground, plant and loading. But it does take a job that used to mean a spreadsheet hunt and shrinks it to a few inputs.

## Variant C — What I find on CAT 3 checks

A few things I see often when checking other people's temporary works designs (in no particular order):

• Plant ground pressures derived from outdated rig data sheets
• Working platform calcs that don't reflect the actual ground beneath, just an assumed CBR
• Propping schemes with no clear sequence for installation or removal
• Hoardings sized for static wind only, no impact / surcharge consideration
• Design briefs that arrive after the design

None of this is unusual. CAT 3 checking is meant to catch it. If you'd like an independent check on a current job, send the calcs, drawings and design brief — fee and programme back promptly.
