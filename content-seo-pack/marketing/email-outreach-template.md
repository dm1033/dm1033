# Email Outreach — Templates

Short, direct, one ask per email. Personalise the first line every time — generic outreach is binned. Avoid attachments on cold sends; add the website link instead.

> **Compliance note:** Cold B2B email to UK businesses is permitted under PECR / UK GDPR using legitimate interests, with a clear opt-out and identified sender. Do not buy mailing lists or send to personal addresses.
