# About Temporary Works Consulting

Temporary Works Consulting is a specialist consultancy providing Temporary Works Design, design checking and construction engineering support to UK and international projects. The service is led personally by David, a UK civil engineer with over 28 years' experience across construction, civil engineering, safety and training.

## Approach
- **Specialist, not generalist.** Temporary works is the focus of the practice.
- **Direct engineer contact.** Briefs, queries and revisions are handled personally.
- **Responsive.** Set up for short-notice and overflow work.
- **Construction-focused.** Designs are written to be built — clear drawings, clear briefs, sensible details.
- **Cautious and qualified.** Designs are produced in accordance with relevant standards and principles, and subject to project-specific information.

## Experience
Over 28 years' experience covering:

- Temporary works design and design checking
- Civil engineering and groundworks
- Construction safety and CDM
- Site-based engineering and supervision
- Construction training

## Standards and principles referenced

Design and consultancy work routinely references, where relevant to a given project:

- BS 5975:2024 — Code of practice for temporary works procedures
- BS EN 1990, 1991, 1993, 1996, 1997 — structural and geotechnical Eurocodes
- BRE 470 — Working Platforms for Tracked Plant
- TWf and CIRIA guidance documents
- ISO 9001, 14001, 45001, 27001, 50001 management system principles

> Specific standards used on any given project depend on scope, ground conditions, plant and contract requirements.
