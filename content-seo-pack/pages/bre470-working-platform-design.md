## H2 — Why BRE 470?

BRE 470 is the established UK reference for granular working platforms. It provides a structured method for sizing platforms for the highest expected ground pressures from piling rigs, cranes and similar plant, based on plant data, granular platform properties and the underlying ground.

## H2 — What's included in a design

- Plant load case derivation from manufacturer data
- Bearing capacity assessment of the underlying ground
- Granular platform thickness sizing to BRE 470 principles
- Edge effects, slopes, sloping ground and reduced cover treatment
- Made-ground or weak-strata considerations
- Recommendations on platform material, compaction and testing
- Inspection regime guidance during construction and operation

## H2 — Typical deliverables

- Calculation pack
- Platform layout drawing or sketch
- Design brief and assumptions register
- Optional independent design check (CAT 2 or CAT 3)
- Working Platform Certificate inputs as required
