# Crane Mat and Piling Mat Design

Design of granular working platforms, crane mats and piling mats for piling rigs, mobile and crawler cranes, and other large tracked or wheeled plant. Designs are produced in accordance with the principles of **BRE 470 — *Working Platforms for Tracked Plant*** and relevant Eurocode geotechnical guidance.

## What is covered

- Piling mats for CFA, rotary, driven, sheet pile and bored piling rigs
- Crane mats for crawler and mobile cranes
- Outrigger pad / spreader pad checks for mobile cranes
- Heavy lift load spreading
- Granular platform thickness checks
- Bearing capacity checks against site-specific ground conditions
- Edge effects, slopes and made-ground considerations

## Typical deliverables

- Platform design calculations to BRE 470 principles
- Sketch / drawing showing platform extent, thickness and material
- Design brief and assumptions
- Recommendations on testing, compaction and inspection
- Independent design check, where required

## Information needed

- Plant data sheet (track length, track width, weights, c.o.g., loads at each track)
- Pile schedule / lift plan / route data
- Ground investigation report (or available factual information)
- Site layout, levels and constraints
