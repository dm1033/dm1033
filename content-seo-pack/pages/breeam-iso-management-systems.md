## Intro
Practical support for the construction-phase elements of BREEAM and for contractors and consultancies maintaining management systems aligned with **ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, ISO 27001** and **ISO 50001**.

> Support is at consultancy level — formal BREEAM assessor or ISO certification body activities are not provided.

## H2 — BREEAM construction-phase support

- Compiling evidence packages for relevant Man / Mat / Wst / Pol credits during construction
- Reviewing site logistics, materials and waste plans against credit requirements
- Liaison with the project's appointed BREEAM Assessor
- Construction-phase site environmental and waste documentation

## H2 — ISO 9001 — Quality

- Quality plan inputs at project level
- ITP preparation and review
- Document control alignment
- Contractor / supplier review approaches

## H2 — ISO 14001 — Environment

- Environmental aspects and impacts at project level
- Waste, materials and pollution control documentation
- Site environmental inspections

## H2 — ISO 45001 — Health & Safety

- Project-level OHS arrangements
- Hazard identification and risk control documentation
- RAMS / safe systems of work alignment with the management system

## H2 — ISO 27001 — Information Security

- Project-level information handling guidance
- Drawings, designs and commercial information controls
- Practical, proportional approaches for SME contractors and consultancies

## H2 — ISO 50001 — Energy

- Site energy use considerations
- Equipment, plant and temporary lighting / power approaches
- Reporting inputs at project level

## H2 — Approach

Support is proportional and project-focused. The aim is workable documentation that integrates with how the contractor actually runs the site, not paperwork that is filed and never used.
