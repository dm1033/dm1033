## H2 — Documents typically produced

- Risk Assessments and Method Statements (RAMS)
- Inspection and Test Plans (ITPs)
- Task-specific safe systems of work
- Lift plans inputs (in support of competent appointed persons)
- Permit-to-load and permit-to-dig documentation supporting BS 5975:2024 procedures
- Construction Phase Plan inputs (CDM 2015) supporting Principal Contractors

## H2 — Typical use cases

- Piling, excavation, propping and lifting operations
- Working platforms and crane bases
- Demolition and façade retention activities
- Hoarding installation and maintenance
- Temporary works installation, alteration and removal

## H2 — Approach

- Documents are kept concise so site teams will read and use them.
- Hazards are linked to controls that are realistic for the site.
- ITPs are aligned with the project quality plan and contract requirements.
- Documentation is designed to support — not duplicate — the contractor's existing systems.
