# Hoarding, Propping and Shoring Design

Design of hoardings, propping schemes, needling and temporary support to existing structures — supporting safe demolition, refurbishment and construction works. Designs are produced in accordance with the relevant Eurocodes, BS 5975:2024 principles and applicable industry guidance.

## Hoardings

- Site hoardings to TWf and contractor standards
- Wind loading checks (BS EN 1991-1-4)
- Foundation / kentledge / fixing checks
- Returns, stiffening posts and gate frames
- Vehicle / impact considerations

## Propping and needling

- Propping schemes for openings, slabs and beams
- Needling sequences for new openings in load-bearing walls
- Adjustable prop layouts and load schedules
- Heavier propping using plate girders, frames or proprietary systems
- Demolition and façade retention concept inputs

## Shoring

- Raking shores and flying shores
- Dead shoring schemes
- Temporary support to listed and historic structures
- Sequencing and removal procedures

## Typical deliverables

- Calculations and drawings
- Design brief and assumptions
- Sequencing instructions
- Independent design check, where required (CAT 2 / CAT 3)

## Information needed

- Existing-structure drawings or survey
- Loads to be supported
- Site constraints (access, services, occupancy)
- Programme and demolition / construction sequence
- Ground information for foundation checks (where applicable)
