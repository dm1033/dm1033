## H1
Excavation Support Design

## Intro
Design of temporary support to excavations — trenches, shafts, basements and battered cuts — for safe construction in a range of ground conditions. Designs reference the relevant Eurocodes (BS EN 1997), BS 5975:2024 and applicable CIRIA guidance, subject to project-specific information.

## H2 — What is covered

- Sheet pile retaining schemes (cantilever and propped)
- Soldier pile and lagging schemes
- Trench boxes and proprietary system reviews
- Battered excavation slope assessment
- Cofferdams and shaft support concepts
- Strut, waling and frame design
- Surcharge and groundwater assessment
- Method of construction sequencing checks

## H2 — Typical deliverables

- Geotechnical and structural calculations
- Drawings showing layout, sections, sequencing and props
- Design brief and assumptions
- Recommendations on monitoring and inspection
- Independent design check arranged where required (CAT 2 / CAT 3)

## H2 — Information needed

- Ground investigation report or factual data
- Excavation depth, plan extent and adjacent constraints
- Surcharge sources (plant, materials, neighbouring buildings)
- Groundwater regime
- Design life and contract standards

## H2 — Considerations

Excavation support is high-risk temporary works. Designs are produced cautiously, with conservative assumptions where information is limited, and revised once site information is confirmed.
